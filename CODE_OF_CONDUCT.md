# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want everyone working on BullRun to feel respected and comfortable
contributing, whatever their experience level.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with teammates who are still learning.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept feedback gracefully and ask questions when something is unclear.
- Credit people for their contributions.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults or discrimination of any kind.
- Personal attacks or deliberately disruptive behaviour.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.

## Enforcement

Concerns can be raised with the team leader, Adwait Misra, through GitHub.
Reports will be handled privately and fairly.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
